# Customer Support Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## 1. Support Channels and Operating Hours
Enterprise Customer Support is available 24/7 via chat, email (support@enterprise.com), and phone line for enterprise clients.

## 2. Response and Resolution Targets
- **VIP Customer Tier:**
  - First Response Time: Under **2 hours**
  - Defective Replacement Dispatch: Within **24 hours**
  - Dedicated account manager assigned.
- **Standard Customer Tier:**
  - First Response Time: Under **24 hours**
  - Resolution Target: Within **48 to 72 hours**

## 3. Escalation for Repeated Product Failures
If a single product category or model exhibits repeated return authorizations exceeding 20% in a given month, our Quality Assurance team initiates an automatic root-cause investigation with the original manufacturer.

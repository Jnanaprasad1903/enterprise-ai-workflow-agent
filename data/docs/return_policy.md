# Enterprise Electronics & Retail Return Policy

## 1. General Return Guidelines
Customers may return most new, unopened items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. All items must be returned with proof of purchase (Order ID or invoice).

## 2. Electronics & Wearables Policy (Laptops, Smart Watches, Headphones)
Because electronic devices require diagnostic testing and factory data wiping upon return, the following specific rules apply:
- **Return Window:** Opened electronic devices (including Smart Watches, Laptops, Headphones, and 4K Monitors) must be returned within **14 calendar days** of delivery.
- **Item Condition:** The device must be in original condition, free of physical damage, water submersion, or customer modifications. All original accessories, chargers, wristbands, and documentation must be included.
- **Restocking Fee:** A standard **15% restocking fee** applies to opened electronics returned for buyer remorse (e.g., changed mind, color preference, size preference).
- **Restocking Fee Waiver for Defects:** If an electronic product is returned due to a **verified manufacturer defect or hardware malfunction** (including defective battery, failure to charge, Bluetooth connectivity failure, or screen display artifacts), the **15% restocking fee is 100% waived**, and the customer receives a full 100% refund.

## 3. Furniture & Office Ergonomics Policy (Chairs, Standing Desks)
- Office chairs and standing desks may be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- If the item was assembled, a **10% disassembly inspection fee** is deducted from the refund unless the return is due to structural part failure or damaged components upon unboxing.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeline
- Once our warehouse receives and inspects the return (typically 2 business days), refunds are credited back to the customer's original payment method within **5 to 7 business days**.
- VIP tier customers receive expedited refunds processed within **24 hours** of warehouse scan.

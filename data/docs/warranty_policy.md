# Enterprise Hardware Warranty Policy

## 1. Standard Warranty Coverage Periods
Every product sold through the enterprise platform is covered by an authentic manufacturer limited warranty:
- **Laptops and Ultrabooks (P001):** 24 months full hardware coverage.
- **Smart Watches & Fitness Trackers (P002):** 12 months coverage covering internal sensors, battery, display, and Bluetooth modem.
- **Ergonomic Chairs and Desks (P003, P007):** 36 to 60 months structural warranty against cylinder failure, frame cracks, or mechanical adjustment failure.
- **Audio & Peripheral Accessories (P004, P005):** 12 months repair or replace warranty against driver failure or key-switch breakdown.

## 2. Covered vs. Non-Covered Issues
- **Covered:** Manufacturing defects, premature battery degradation (capacity falling below 70% within warranty period), dead pixels (>3), firmware boot failures, structural component breakages under normal use.
- **Excluded:** Accidental liquid damage (unless specified waterproof rating was observed without exceeding depth), screen glass drops, unauthorized motherboard modifications, or cosmetic wear and tear.

## 3. Warranty Claim Process
1. Contact customer support with the Order ID and product serial number.
2. An RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number will be generated within 12 hours.
3. Prepaid return shipping labels are provided for all valid warranty service requests.

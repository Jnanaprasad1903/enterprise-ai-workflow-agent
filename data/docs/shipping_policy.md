# Enterprise Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods and Delivery Speeds
- **Standard Ground Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days across all domestic regions. Flat rate of $500 for orders under $50,000.
- **Expedited Express Shipping:** 1 to 2 business days. Flat rate of $1,500.
- **Free Enterprise Shipping:** Orders with an aggregate total exceeding **$50,000** automatically qualify for complimentary Standard Ground Shipping. VIP tier clients receive complimentary Expedited Shipping on all orders.

## 2. Tracking and Order Confirmation
Customers receive an automated dispatch notification with carrier tracking link (FedEx/BlueDart) as soon as the package leaves our central distribution center.

## 3. Damaged or Lost in Transit
- If a parcel arrives with visible exterior damage or broken seals, the customer must report it to Customer Support within **48 hours** of delivery.
- Photographic evidence of the outer carton and damaged hardware must be attached.
- Our claims department will issue an immediate replacement order within 24 hours of claim verification.
